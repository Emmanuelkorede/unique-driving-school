01 The starting point

Your first drive doesn’t have to feel intimidating.

If you have never driven before, or the road makes you nervous, that is a normal place to start — not a reason to give up.

Many learners put off learning to drive because they assume it is something other people are naturally good at. It isn’t. It is a set of skills, and skills are taught — one manageable step at a time.

At Unique Driving School, you begin with the controls and the basics, then move out onto the road only when you are ready for it. Your instructor will not rush you, will explain rather than just correct, and will repeat anything until it feels natural. Lessons are booked around your schedule, and we will talk you through the driver’s licensing process so you know what is required and what comes next.

  • No previous driving experience required
  • Patient, professional instructors
  • Lesson times that fit around your life
  • Guidance on the driver’s licensing process
